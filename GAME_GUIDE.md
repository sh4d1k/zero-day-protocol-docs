# Zero Day Protocol — Operative Field Manual & Gameplay Guide

Welcome, Operative. This guide provides a non-developer overview of the core gameplay loop, systems, and tactical strategies in **Zero Day Protocol**.

---

## 🎯 The Core Mission
In **Zero Day Protocol**, you play as an elite freelance net-runner in a dystopian cyberpunk network war. Your goal is to infiltrate corporate infrastructure, crack encrypted databanks, deploy zero-day exploits, and extract valuable digital intelligence while evading Intrusion Countermeasure Electronics (ICE).

## 🧩 Key Game Systems

### 1. 🌐 The Network Router (Grid Infiltration)
* **Goal**: Route transmission packets from your terminal node to the target core.
* **Mechanics**: Rotate and align signal conduits to establish uninterrupted high-bandwidth pipelines.
* **Hazards**: Avoid ICE alarm triggers that accelerate detection timers.

### 2. 🔐 Cipher Modules (Cryptographic Decoding)
* **Goal**: Crack dynamic binary/hexadecimal security codes.
* **Mechanics**: Analyze cipher patterns, frequency distributions, and algorithmic rules to decipher encrypted payloads before the security threshold triggers a lockdown.

### 3. ⚡ Hardware & Software Upgrades
Invest your extracted credits into terminal enhancements:
* **Overclocked Processing Cores**: Boost your execution speed and extend breach timers.
* **Buffer Memory Expansion**: Unlock additional undo/recovery slots during routing puzzles.
* **Decryption Algorithms**: Automate preliminary cipher analysis for faster solving.
* **Decentralized Mining Rigs**: Passively generate background credits over time (tick economy).

### 4. ☁️ Cloud Sync & Operative Alias
* **Custom Alias**: Establish your hacker codename on the global leaderboard.
* **Cross-Device Sync**: Sign in with your Google account to automatically preserve your upgrades, credits, and unlocked sectors across all Android devices.

## 💡 Top Operative Tips
1. **Never Rush Blindly**: Study the grid before making your first route connection.
2. **Prioritize Mining Upgrades**: Early investments in automated mining yield substantial passive dividends for high-end gear later.
3. **Keep Your Terminal Calibrated**: Use the Settings menu to adjust sound, haptics, and interface scaling for maximum operational efficiency.
