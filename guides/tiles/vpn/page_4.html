🚫 THE INGESTION PROTOCOL

A still-cloaked packet cannot enter the destination server (Exit Node [E]). Because its handshake headers are encrypted, the server fails to recognize the transmission. The packet will pass straight through or crash into the perimeter wall behind the server!

• CRITICAL FAILURE: Do not route cloaked packets directly into the Exit Node without clearing the tunnel cloak.
💡 HOW TO UNCLOAK

Passing through a second VPN Node toggles the encrypted tunnel OFF, returning the packet to its normal readable state so it can successfully deliver payload intelligence to the Exit Server.

• SOLUTION: [Start] → [VPN 1 (Cloak)] → [Bypass Walls] → [VPN 2 (Uncloak)] → [Exit Server].
