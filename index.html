Privacy Policy Summary

Last Updated: September 5, 2026 | Developer: MTX Games (Sri Lanka)


Zero Day Protocol respects your privacy. We collect only the data required to provide cloud save synchronization, game features, and stability:

1. Data Collected

  • Authentication Data: Email address, Display Name, and User ID (UID) when signing in via Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth.
  • Game State: Progress, level completions, upgrades, high scores, and in-game credit balances stored securely on Firebase.
  • Crash & Performance Telemetry: Diagnostic logs to resolve software crashes and improve performance.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Cloud database, authentication, and hosting.
  • Google AdMob: In-game advertisements (rewarded ads and banners).
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchase processing (we do not process or store credit cards directly).

3. Permissions

Zero Day Protocol requires INTERNET for cloud sync and VIBRATE for haptic feedback. We do NOT record, capture, or store microphone/audio data.
