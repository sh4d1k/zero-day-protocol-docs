The Three Core Gameplay Pillars

Route the packets, crack the cipher gateways that guard some nodes, and feed everything you win back into your terminal and your data cores.

PILLAR 01 // ROUTER ENGINE

🌐 Network Grid Infiltration

Place deflectors and other tiles on the grid, then launch data packets from the Start Node and get them to the server before your signal integrity drains. Packets carry momentum and cannot steer — your tiles do the steering. The OVERCLOCK GEAR speeds the run up or slows time down.

Boot Origin · 29 Levels Overclock Gear Deflectors & Shift Registers VPN Tunnels & ICE Wormholes & Splitters EMP Mines & Fields Sentry ICE Timed Gates Colour Spoofers Level Leaderboards
PILLAR 02 // CIPHER GATEWAY

🔐 Cipher Security Gateway

Some nodes sit behind a cipher gateway: crack its numeric PIN code to get in. Each guess shows which digits are right and in place, and which are right but misplaced — and every miss costs a proxy. Security modules make it harder; attack them to break them down, but a missed attack costs a proxy too.

Matrix Shuffle Keypad Hidden History Masked Feedback Module Attacks Proxy Refills
PILLAR 03 // DECRYPTOR LAB

🧬 Data Core Synthesis & Economy

Load data cores into the Decryptor and merge two, three or four of them into one bigger core over time, then extract it with a spin. Keep cores in your stash or deconstruct them for DarkCoin. The DarkCoin miner and the proxy scanner keep working while you're away.

The Cyber Globe & Multiplayer Raids

The network is a world. Pick your city on the holographic Cyber Globe, build a base of your own, and break into other operatives' bases.

WORLD // CYBER GLOBE

🌍 Your City Hub

A holographic globe with a connection sequence, a city unlock ceremony and a cell grid over each city. Boot Origin City is where every operative starts; more cities wait on the globe, locked until you earn your way in.

DEFENCE // GRID

🛡️ Defence Grid

Design and deploy your own base for other operatives to raid. New operatives start with a deployed starter base, and you can open your active base in a full read-only view.

ATTACK // RAIDS

⚔️ Raids & Battle Logs

FIND OPPONENT, read the scout card, and break into their base for Ɖ DarkCoin. You get two attempts, and a ROOT clear lets you try to extract one of their data cores. When you are raided, an under-attack alert fires and a shield covers you afterwards. Battle logs on the globe keep score both ways.

Privacy Policy Summary

Last Updated: November 06, 2026 | Developer & Trader: MTX Games (Sri Lanka)


Zero Day Protocol respects your privacy. We collect only the data required to provide cloud save synchronization, game features, and stability:

1. Data Collected

  • Authentication Data: Email address, Display Name, and User ID (UID) when signing in via Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth.
  • Game State: Progress, level completions, upgrades, high scores, and in-game credit balances stored securely on Firebase.
  • Crash & Performance Telemetry: Diagnostic logs to resolve software crashes and optimize performance.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Cloud database, authentication, and hosting.
  • Supabase: Competitive leaderboards and server-side raid verification.
  • Google AdMob: In-game advertisements (rewarded ads and banners).
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchase processing (we do not process or store credit cards directly).

3. Permissions & Trader Status

Zero Day Protocol requires INTERNET for cloud sync and VIBRATE for tactile haptic feedback. We do NOT record, capture, or store microphone/audio data. MTX Games complies with the EU Digital Services Act (DSA) as a registered commercial Trader for in-app purchases.
