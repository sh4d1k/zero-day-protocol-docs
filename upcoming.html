Roadmap — the big picture

Zero Day Protocol is a cyberpunk routing puzzle about a voice on the line, a packet, and the network beyond Boot Origin. Here is where it is going.

  1. DONE
    Closed testing
    The first operatives, the campaign, raids and the Cyber Globe.
  2. NOW
    Open testing
    Live on Google Play in six countries; a new tutorial run in testing.
  3. NEXT
    Google Play launch
    The full release, worldwide — currently under Google's review.
  4. LATER
    Multiplayer season
    Cipher-locked bases, more base tiles, raid replays, player-built levels.
  5. LATER
    New cities & story
    Nexus and Propediem, the Boot Origin city map, and more of the Handler's story.

We're also exploring a Web3 layer for the game's economy — nothing is decided yet, and there are no tokens or NFTs today.
