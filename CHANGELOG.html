v1.1.5

Internal testing
PROTOCOL: HARDENED UPLINK
NOV 2026

A fix release. It also carries everything from v1.1.4, which never left internal testing — closed and open testers go straight from v1.1.3 to here.

New
  • Decryptor slots 3 and 4 unlock again when you clear their levels. Saves that missed an unlock are repaired automatically when the game loads.
  • Defence: view your active base — the deployed grid now opens in a full read-only view.
  • Black Market: when the supply line can't be reached, you get a retry button instead of an empty shelf.
  • Google Play Games sign-in: on a fresh install your operative loads by itself, with no prompt. A Play Games profile that already belongs to another operative is refused with a clear message.
  • Handler briefing: progress dots in place of the slide counter.
  • Navigation bar: the raised HOME button sits lower when you're away from the home screen.
Fixes
  • Fixed a rare crash in the graphics engine that could close the game on some phones.
Pre-registration reward
  • Founder bundle for operatives who pre-register on Google Play: ⟠ 450 QC, Ɖ 60,000 DC and 50 proxies, delivered when the game launches.
In testing — may change
  • A short "AD" countdown ring before an ad plays, so it never jumps in unannounced.
  • Sign in with Google straight from the first-launch briefing.
  • Features that open as you clear campaign levels.

v1.1.4

Internal testing only
PROTOCOL: HANDLER BRIEFING
07 NOV 2026

Held back on internal testing — a graphics crash was found during testing. Everything below reaches closed and open testers with v1.1.5.

New
  • First-launch handler briefing: eight illustrated slides, the handler's lines typed out on screen, for new operatives only.
  • Redesigned home screen with an operative header and a new navigation bar: Defence, Lab, Home, Upgrades, Shop.
  • Miner and proxy boosts now run side by side, each on its own timer.
  • Reworked Upgrades screen with live countdown timers.
  • Guest accounts: a clear warning before a guest account is left behind for a signed-in operative — the guest account is then deleted.
In testing — may change
  • The operative room: an isometric home base with your server rack, miner tower and cloning tubes.
  • Six distinct data-core looks, and a history for every core.

v1.1.3

Closed testing
PROTOCOL: INCIDENT LOG
27 OCT 2026

The tutorial rework, levels delivered from the server, and fresh level leaderboards. Your campaign progress is kept.

New
  • New tutorial Level 3 — RELAY // INCIDENT LOG: targeting cells, energy and the integrity hub. Boot Origin now has 29 levels, so the level numbers after it move up.
  • Level 1 is simpler — the energy block moved out of it.
  • Level 2 is reworked around the Overclock Gear, the new name for the packet speed control.
  • Levels can now be updated without an app update.
  • New level leaderboards with a fairer score and clear times to the millisecond. Every board started fresh.
  • Field Manual windows in a level now show only the pages that level needs.
Now on for everyone
  • The Cyber Globe, full-screen mode, vector tile icons, tile glide, packet effects and packet cosmetics.

Closed testing
