NOV 2026 // PROTOCOL: HARDENED UPLINK

Hardened Uplink: The Fix Release

A release about fixing problems. It carries everything from v1.1.4 too, and is live in Google Play Closed testing and Open testing (Brazil, India, Indonesia, the Philippines, Romania and Sri Lanka) — closed and open testers go straight from v1.1.3 to here.

•
STABILITY Graphics engine crash fixed: a rare crash in the graphics engine that could close the game on some phones.
•
DECRYPTOR LAB Decryptor slots 3 and 4 unlock again when you clear their levels. Saves that missed an unlock are repaired automatically when the game loads.
•
DEFENCE View your active base: the deployed grid opens in a full read-only view.
•
BLACK MARKET A retry button instead of an empty shelf when the supply line can't be reached.
•
SIGN-IN Google Play Games: on a fresh install your operative loads by itself, with no prompt. A Play Games profile that already belongs to another operative is refused with a clear message.
•
INTERFACE Polish: progress dots in the handler briefing.
•
PRE-REGISTRATION Founder Bundle for operatives who pre-register on Google Play: ⟠ 450 QC, Ɖ 60,000 DC and 50 proxies, delivered when the game launches.
•
IN TESTING An "AD" countdown ring before an ad plays, sign-in with Google from the first-launch briefing, and features that open as you clear campaign levels — these may change.

The Handler's Briefing & a New Home

Held back on internal testing after a graphics crash turned up during testing. Everything below reaches closed and open testers with v1.1.5.

•
STORY First-launch handler briefing: eight illustrated slides, the handler's lines typed out on screen, for new operatives only.
•
INTERFACE Redesigned home screen with an operative header and a new navigation bar: Defence, Lab, Home, Upgrades, Shop.
•
ECONOMY Miner and proxy boosts now run side by side, each on its own timer, and the Upgrades screen is reworked with live countdown timers.
•
ACCOUNTS Guest accounts: a clear warning before a guest account is left behind for a signed-in operative — the guest account is then deleted.
•
IN TESTING The operative room — an isometric home base with your server rack, miner tower and cloning tubes — and six distinct data-core looks with a history for every core. These may change.

13 OCT 2026 // GLOBE & RAIDS LIVE 21 OCT

The Cyber Globe & Multiplayer Raids

The build that brought the Cyber Globe and multiplayer raids — switched on for players on 21 October 2026.

•
WORLD HUB The Cyber Globe: a holographic globe to pick your city, with a connection sequence, a city unlock ceremony, a network view and a cell grid over each city.
•
DEFENCE Defence Grid: build and deploy your own base for other operatives to raid. New operatives start with a deployed starter base.
•
MULTIPLAYER Raids: FIND OPPONENT, a scout card, two attempts, data-core extraction from a beaten base, and an under-attack alert with a shield afterwards. Battle logs on the globe list your attacks and the raids on your base.
•
INTEGRITY Integrity v2 for everyone: integrity drains slower at high speed and faster at low speed, the HUD shows the baseline and your bonuses on two lines, and the run ends if integrity reaches zero with nothing delivered.
•
INTERFACE Themed confirmation boxes, the redesigned handler message box, full-screen mode and interface font themes.
