DISPATCH // v1.1.0 ACTIVE DEV BUILD // BUILD 11
TARGET MILESTONE: SKIA GPU ENGINE

⚡ GPU Board Rendering, Tactical Glide Physics & Vector Iconography

Major modernization phase transitioning the puzzle engine from composite component rendering to locked 60 FPS GPU canvas execution (React Native Skia), introducing tactile motion interpolation, and refining tactical routing rules.

•
GRAPHICS React Native Skia GPU Board Engine: Transferred packet transit, Sentry scans, particle trails, and full grid tiles to dedicated hardware-accelerated Skia rendering, eliminating frame jitter during high-density multi-packet bursts.
•
CONTROLS Tactical Tile Glide & Motion Physics: Introduced 150ms smooth ease-out gliding for tap-moves and tap-swaps, animated 220ms flight paths between inventory tray and board, and responsive drag-preview snap-back for rejected drop attempts.
•
GESTURES Direct Drag-to-Swap: Operatives can now drag a placed board tile directly onto another occupied tile to trigger an instant location swap, protected by a 500ms safety buffer preventing accidental busy-state corruption.
•
ART Vector Neon Tile Iconography: Replaced placeholder glyphs with bespoke neon line vector icon paths across all tile families, visual previews, and tray slots.
•
ENGINE Catch-Up Ticks Clock Synchronization: Late engine ticks are now replayed (up to 5 per fire) rather than dropped, ensuring Timed Gates, Sentries, EMP fuses, and integrity decay maintain true real-time velocity regardless of device load.
•
ICE RULES Sentry Scanning Beams & EMP Timed Charges: Red Sentries actively project a forward scan beam that neutralizes uncloaked packets; payloads entering an EMP cell instantly arm a 500ms timed charge that locks and detonates.
•
HUD Integrity v2 & Throttle Decay Scaling: Decay rate scales dynamically with transmission throttle (0.5× at full speed, 2× at low speed). Upgraded in-game HUD to display independent baseline survival (N/1000) and bonus extraction (X/800) counters.
•
COSMETICS Operative Loadout & Custom Packet Trails: Added operative loadout interface enabling operatives to equip custom packet heads and particle trail effects.
