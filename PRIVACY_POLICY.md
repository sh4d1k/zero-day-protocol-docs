# Privacy Policy for Zero Day Protocol

**Last Updated: September 2, 2026**

MTX Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Zero Day Protocol** mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information to provide, maintain, and improve our Service.

### Types of Data Collected

#### A. Personal & Account Data
* **Account Authentication**: When authenticating via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we collect your Email address, Display Name, and unique User Identifier (UID).
* **Game Progress & Analytics**: High scores, game statistics, currency balance, operational node states, and unlocked upgrades stored securely in Firebase Firestore.
* **Anonymous Guest Mode**: If you play without logging in, a randomized local/anonymous token is generated solely to persist your local game progression.

#### B. Device Data & Diagnostics
* **Diagnostics & Stability**: Device model, operating system version, performance metrics, and crash diagnostics to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

---

## 2. Permissions

* **Internet Access (`INTERNET`)**: Required for cloud synchronization, leaderboards, authentication, and game data retrieval.
* **Vibration (`VIBRATE`)**: Used for tactile haptic feedback during interactive terminal operations and puzzle completion.
* *(Note: Zero Day Protocol does **not** record, capture, or store microphone/audio data).*

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We partner with trusted third-party service providers to power specific game functionality:

* **Google Play Services & Firebase (Google LLC)**: Used for cloud database synchronization, user authentication, and performance analytics. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google AdMob (Google LLC)**: Used to display in-game advertisements (Banners, Interstitials, Rewarded Videos). AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (GAID) in accordance with Google policies. [AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
* **Google Play In-App Billing**: All payment transactions are processed directly by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Security

* Your authentication and game progress data are stored securely in Google Cloud / Firebase and retained as long as your account remains active.
* We utilize industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to secure all data transmissions.

---

## 5. Account and Data Deletion

You have the full right to delete your operative account and all associated data at any time:

* **In-App**: Navigate to **Settings > Account > Delete Account** to purge your profile immediately.
* **Via Email**: Send a deletion request to **`cybershadic7@gmail.com`** with your registered Google email or Operative ID. We will permanently purge your data from our database within 30 days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is designed for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
