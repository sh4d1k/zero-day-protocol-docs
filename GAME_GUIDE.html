The Core Mission

In Zero Day Protocol, you operate as an elite freelance net-runner in a dystopian cyberpunk network war. Your directive is to penetrate corporate security infrastructure, crack encrypted databanks, deploy zero-day exploits, and extract high-value digital intelligence before Intrusion Countermeasure Electronics (ICE) can trace your physical rig.

• PRIMARY OPERATIONAL GOAL: Deliver transmission data packets to target mainframe nodes with maximum integrity retention, cracking cryptographic gateways and synthesizing extracted data cores into permanent hardware upgrades.

Key Operational Systems

🌐 1. Network Router Engine

The puzzle grid operates on dynamic packet momentum. Packets smoothly accelerate through conduits, and tiles entering busy states (packet center transit) temporarily lock against displacement.

  • Sentry ICE Beams: Red Sentries project continuous forward scan beams; amber Sentries rest and turn.
