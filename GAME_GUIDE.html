The Briefing

You found a connection you were not meant to find. On the other end is a handler — he has been waiting for you, and he needs to know whether you can use it. He installs a new connection to your terminal and sends you into Boot Origin City, the network every operative starts in.

The first nodes are training. They do not stay that way: on the second the handler nearly burns himself out freezing time to save you, and by the third and the incident log shows that someone set a node to fire before you could touch the board — someone who knew you were coming.

• YOUR JOB: get every data packet from the Start Node to the server with as much signal integrity left as you can, crack the cipher gateways that guard some nodes, and turn what you win into a stronger terminal and bigger data cores.

The Infiltration Loop

Every node you breach follows the same five steps:

01

Pick a Node on the Cyber Globe

Tap INITIATE CONNECTION, open your city on the holographic Cyber Globe and choose the next node. Boot Origin City holds 29 levels; other cities on the globe stay locked until you earn your way in.

Crack the Cipher Gateway

Some nodes are guarded by a cipher: guess the numeric PIN code before your proxies run out. Each guess shows which digits are right and in place, and which are right but in the wrong slot.

↓

Route the Packets

Place tiles from your inventory, launch packets from the Start Node and steer them to the server past walls, ICE, mines and Sentries. Use the OVERCLOCK GEAR to speed the run up or slow time down.

↓
04

Score Your Clearance

Your final signal integrity (0–1000) sets your clearance tier and stars — ROOT, USER, GUEST or RESTRICTED — and decides your Ɖ DarkCoin, XP and ⟠ QC rewards. Your score also goes to the level's leaderboard.

↓
05

Reinvest

Merge data cores in the Decryptor, upgrade your DarkCoin miner and proxy scanner, build your Defence Grid — then go raid another operative.

Signal Integrity & Clearance

Integrity starts at 1000 and drains once transmission begins. Every packet you deliver and every energy block you collect adds to it: deliveries share up to 600, energy blocks share up to 200 (a level with no energy blocks gives no energy bonus). When the last packet is delivered or lost, the total is capped at 1000 — then every energy block you missed costs its share a second time, and delivering fewer than half your packets drops you to 10.

ROOT ★★★900 – 1000Full rewards.
USER ★★600 – 89960% of the level's rewards.
GUEST ★300 – 59930% of the level's rewards.
RESTRICTED1 – 299The level clears and the next one opens, but there are no rewards.
CONNECTION LOST0No packet delivered, or integrity drained to zero with nothing delivered — retry.

Rewards stack by star: your first star pays 30% of the level's rewards, the second another 30%, the third the last 40%. Replaying a level without a new star pays 1% (only with 2 stars or more). ⟠ QC is paid once, the first time you reach 3 stars.

⚙️ The Overclock Gear

The OVERCLOCK GEAR beside the board sets the packet speed — and how fast integrity drains. Push it up and packets run up to 3.2× faster while integrity drains at half the rate; pull it down to slow time and buy yourself a window, at up to twice the drain. In Settings you can move the gear to the side that suits your hand (OVERCLOCK GEAR SIDE).

Key Operational Systems

🌐 1. Network Router Engine

Packets carry momentum and cannot steer; your tiles do the steering.

  • Placing tiles: drag a tile from the inventory, tap a tile then a cell, or tap a cell then a tile. Tap a placed tile then another to swap them; double-tap a placed tile to return it.
  • Launching: the integrity hub shows one box per packet — a box marked > launches on its own, a box marked II waits for your tap on the Start Node.
  • Tiles you will meet: deflectors, U-turn pads, Shift Registers, Flip-Flops, VPN tunnels, Colour Spoofers, Wormholes, Splitters, Buffers, keys and Timed Gates.
  • The hazards: walls, Basic and Stateful ICE, EMP mines and fields, and Sentry ICE scan beams.

🔐 2. Cipher Gateway

Crack the PIN code before your proxies run out — every wrong guess costs one.

  • Feedback: green for a right digit in the right slot, yellow for a right digit in the wrong slot.
  • Security modules: Matrix Shuffle rearranges the keypad after every attempt; others hide your history, mask the feedback or allow repeated digits.
  • Attacks: attack a module to wear it down and knock it out — a missed attack costs a proxy.
  • Out of proxies? Refill with an ad or a ticket.

🧬 3. Decryptor Lab & Upgrades

Reinvest your spoils into your terminal:

  • Merges: load two, three or four data cores into the Decryptor's merge slots; they merge into one bigger core over time. Slots 3 and 4 unlock as you clear their levels.
  • Extraction: the finished core comes out with an extraction spin — re-spin with ⟠ QC or an ad.
  • Stash: keep cores in a stash of up to 16 slots, or deconstruct a core for Ɖ DarkCoin.
  • Upgrades: raise the DarkCoin miner's capacity and speed and the proxy scanner's capacity and scan speed. Research runs on a timer; boosts run side by side, each on its own timer.

⚔️ 4. Cyber Globe, Defence Grid & Raids

Asynchronous multiplayer on the globe:

  • Defence Grid: design and deploy a base for other operatives to raid. New operatives start with a deployed starter base.
  • Raids: FIND OPPONENT, read the scout card and play their base for Ɖ DarkCoin. You get two attempts; a ROOT clear lets you try to extract one of their data cores.
  • Defence: an under-attack alert warns you while you are raided, and a shield covers you afterwards.
  • Battle logs: your attacks and the raids on your base, on the globe.

☁️ 5. Profile, Leaderboards & Loadout

Your operative, saved and on the boards:

  • Cloud save: sign in with Google or Google Play Games and your progress, upgrades and balances follow you to any Android device.
  • Leaderboards: each level has its own board, ranked by score, with clear times to the millisecond.
  • Identity: your operative alias, player titles and the Operative Dossier.
  • Loadout: pick your packet's head and trail on the LOADOUT screen.

🛒 6. Shop, Black Market & VIP

Optional ways to speed things up:

  • Shop: ⟠ Quantum Crystal packs, ad-skip ticket bundles, and crystal-to-Ɖ DarkCoin exchanges.
  • Black Market: special offers that come and go.
  • VIP: skips ads, an extra research queue, and +10% DarkCoin mining and proxy scan speed.
  • Bigger spends ask first with a confirmation box.

Currencies & Resources

  • Ɖ DarkCoin (DC) — the everyday currency: level rewards, the miner, raids, upgrades. The miner can be harvested once it holds at least 100 Ɖ.
  • ⟠ Quantum Crystals (QC) — the premium currency: first-time 3-star clears, crystal packs, re-spins and instant finishes.
  • Proxies — your cipher attempts. The proxy scanner refills them over time.
  • Tickets — skip an ad without watching it, or refill your proxies instantly.
  • Data cores — stored data, measured in MB and GB, merged in the Decryptor.

Top Operative Tips

TIP 02 // HARVEST THE MINER OFTEN: The DarkCoin miner keeps working while you are away, but it stops at its capacity. Harvest it and upgrade its capacity so nothing goes to waste.
